Delete your TOWY TAXI account

You can ask us to delete your account and all your booking data at any time.

How

Email us from any address with the subject Delete my account and include the mobile number or Google email you signed in with.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

Your sign-in account, your name, phone number, addresses, map pins, ride history, ratings and notification settings. We do this within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we may keep

If our taxi licence conditions require us to keep booking records for a set time, we keep only those records for that period and then delete them.
