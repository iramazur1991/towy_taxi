TOWY TAXI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

TOWY TAXI, Carmarthenshire, Wales. We are the data controller for the information you give us when booking. Contact: iramazur1991@gmail.com

What we collect

Your name and mobile number; pick-up and destination addresses and postcodes; map pins if you choose to drop them (and your location only if you tap “Use my location”); ride date, time, passengers and notes; the price, the driver, and any rating you leave.

Why

To arrange, confirm and carry out your booking, to contact you about it, to show you your ride history, and to keep booking records that licensing rules require us to keep. The legal basis is performing the contract you ask us for, and our legal obligations.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud, UK/EU region). To confirm your number, Google sends you a one-time text code. Maps are shown with OpenStreetMap and postcodes are checked with postcodes.io — they receive only the postcode or map area, not your name. Driver alerts are sent through ntfy.sh and contain only a booking reference and time, never your name, number or address. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Only TOWY TAXI and our licensed drivers.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for 12 months after the ride (or longer if our licence conditions require it), then deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within one month. To delete your account see Delete your account. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
