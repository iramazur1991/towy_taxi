TOWY TAXI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you book a ride we collect your name, mobile number, pick-up and destination addresses and postcodes, the date and time of the ride, number of passengers and any notes you add. After a ride you may leave a star rating.

Why we collect it

Only to arrange, confirm and carry out your taxi booking, to contact you about it, and to keep a history of your rides that you can view in the app.

Where it is stored

Booking data is stored securely with Google Firebase (Google Cloud, London region). Your name, address and postcode may also be remembered on your own device to make the next booking faster.

Who sees it

Only TOWY TAXI and its drivers. We never sell or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted, unless the law requires longer.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Contact us and we will act within 30 days.
